Deligato is a Capital Intelligence platform for founders and growing companies. It is not an investor directory.

Understand your company

Company profile, financial health that adapts to your financial state, and your company record in one place.

Become capital ready

A free readiness score, then a full Capital Readiness assessment with strengths, gaps and a ranked improvement plan.

Find the right capital

Venture capital, angels, grants and government programmes, revenue-based finance, venture debt, bank and working-capital finance: the right kind of capital comes before any list of names.

Understand why it fits

Every match is explained criterion by criterion, with the source of each fact. Unknown is never counted as a fit.

Execute the raise

Shortlist, pipeline, outreach drafts you edit and send yourself, and a data room checklist for your stage.

Questions

Is Deligato an investor directory?

No. Deligato starts with your company: it shows how capital-ready you are, which kinds of capital fit, and why each provider fits, with the source of every fact. Then it helps you run the raise.

Does Deligato raise money for me?

No. We help you with fundraising. We never fundraise for you. You decide who to contact, edit every message and send it yourself.

Who is Deligato for?

Startups raising capital, and established businesses looking for growth or working capital.

Where does the capital provider information come from?

From our research database, built from providers' own websites, official registers, licensed data and public sources. Every fact carries a source label and the date it was last checked.
